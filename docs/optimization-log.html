Findings on the critical path

Method

Rust: slotmap 1.1.1 with Criterion. Mojo: this port with std.benchmark. Both time one pass over n elements (1K, 100K, 1M) for insert, shuffled get, shuffled remove, iteration over a half-empty map, and re-insertion into freed slots. Mojo/Rust below 1.0 means Mojo is faster. Shared runners are noisy: treat anything under about 15% as noise.

Local micro-benchmarks (best of 20 runs on an M2 laptop) were used to pick directions; every claim of a change in the tables above comes from the CI run linked in its round. The raw results of each CI run are kept under bench/history/, and this page is generated from them by bench/build_log.py.
